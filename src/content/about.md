I'm Marek — principal engineer, AI researcher, and product builder.

I like working at the intersection of research, systems, and real-world
applications — especially when the path isn't clear yet. Something bothers
me, I go deep enough to understand the problem, hack toward a solution,
and care whether the result is actually useful. Then I write down what I
learned.

Research when needed. Build when possible. Ship what matters.

## Background

I started my journey into computer science at 15, coding in C++ without
internet access, in good old fashioned
[Bloodshed C++](https://www.bloodshed.net/). Nearly 20 years later, I have
a Master's degree in Computer Science and Statistics from the University of
Economics in Bratislava. I've done machine learning and built distributed
systems for early to mid-stage startups. Right now I'm a Senior AI and
Machine Learning Researcher at [ESET](https://www.eset.com), combining
cutting-edge AI with cybersecurity.

## Professional interests

* High-performance and distributed computing
* Reverse engineering and vulnerability exploitation
* Secure coding
